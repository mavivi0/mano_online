I'm a Quantitative Researcher at Balyasny Asset Management, working on AI research including agentic systems and domain-adapted language models for finance.

Previously, I was a Senior Research Scientist at Lifion by ADP, and I hold a PhD in Mathematics from the University of Illinois at Chicago, advised by Lev Reyzin, and a Master's in Mathematics from IISER Thiruvananthapuram.

My research interests are in machine learning and optimization.
